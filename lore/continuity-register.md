# Continuity register — Sophia / Saafiya's Revenge

**Updated: 28 November 2026.** This is a deliberately unresolved decision ledger. It is *not* an error list requiring immediate cleanup. Fragmented or mutually exclusive proposals are retained here so future writing can join, replace or reject them **explicitly**.

## Status conventions and source precedence

- **Recorded legacy:** a detail asserted as established in the source Hero-shooter story bible; preserved exactly in the source snapshot. A newer proposal can create a conflict without silently rewriting legacy.
- **Recent direction:** a director-described preference or character fact from subsequent discussions, strongest as a *creative intention* rather than proof that a screenplay/gameplay asset has changed.
- **Proposal / assistant interpretation:** a possible scene, connective explanation or character insight. **Do not treat as canon without adoption.**
- **Historical inspiration:** the director's lived recollection of Dark Mountain and other influences; distinct from independently documented history and from fictional Ambrosia.
- **Implemented:** a verified game/repository feature. Implementation status does not establish fictional chronology unless the story design says so.

**Source policy:** Retain original versions even when later ones disagree. A conversation alone does not rewrite gameplay source, art or earlier Markdown. The archived Hero-shooter files are frozen verbatim under [source-hero-shooter](source-hero-shooter/), even though their old relative Markdown links still reflect their source repository structure.

## Crisis / story-act architecture — deliberately not solved

**Sequence candidates currently coexisting, not a single stitched timeline:**

A. **Legacy Johannesburg/Fayeem lead:** Saafiya visits Fayeem's family, notices an abrupt departure, receives an anonymous Copperlane email, leaves in the Mustang, and searches for him. There is a Russian-operative/secret-family coercion storyline.

B. **KZN-first 2021 direction:** origin around KwaZulu-Natal unrest; Saafiya encounters roadblocks, dangerous crowds and mall access issues. A newer version has Russian deliver important information *in person* that draws her toward Fayeem.

C. **C / Home & More Sunday opening:** a different viewpoint character **C** is with Mr and Mrs Chen, Wei and their workers on L1 of Pine Ridge Mall; a closed-shop/receiving crisis develops. Do not assume Saafiya is C, or that every Chen-family episode is played by Saafiya.

D. **Native Godot implemented stages:** Upper Pineridge residential approach -> House One/barricaded house -> existing feeder road/main stage. These are implemented **game-stage relationships**; their ultimate chronology relative to A–C remains open.

E. **Longer criminal/political branch:** Russian's influence, Fayeem's history, Ernest Meintjies/"Domino", Justin, a taxi encounter, investigations, factional shifts, and the terrible possible "But None of Us Will Win" ending. Their exact links and final states remain unwritten.

**Critical contradictions not to erase:**
- Does the Pine Ridge disaster happen in KwaZulu-Natal while Copperlane lies in Johannesburg? Is one a flash-forward or a separate chapter?
- Is Fayeem the same person any earlier draft calls Mr. Fahim? Never merge on name similarity alone.
- Who is playable C, what is C's actual connection to Saafiya, and does a prior code asset blur two characters for implementation reasons only?
- Was the restaurant/fuel-station manageress career established before the unrest, and is the Mustang present in both geographic treatments?
- When and how does Saafiya learn of the other woman/child? She does **not** initially know everything Fayeem and Russian know.
- What knowledge and motive bring Domino, Russian, the Chen family and Saafiya into the same physical place?
- How much of the later morality-choice branch is optional gameplay and how much is definite biography?

## How to resolve a thread later

When a decision is explicitly adopted:
1. Record the **new decision, date, and what it supersedes** (including whether old scenes remain flashbacks, rejected drafts or alternate branches).
2. Link the precise source section and edit the **unified notes**; keep conflicting historical drafts readable under **source-hero-shooter**.
3. Update chapter scripts, dialogue, character sheets and art **only if** the decision affects them.
4. Verify factual claims about implementation against the current Godot files; do not describe prose-only concepts as working gameplay.
5. Leave remaining open questions open. There is no requirement to make every fragment agree immediately.
