## 1. CORRECTION — ownership of the inexpensive Rossi .38 Special

**Earlier recovery error:** The *fourth-pass audit* and parts of the consolidated notes assigned an inexpensive **Rossi .38 Special** to **Heather's mother / Saafiya's maternal grandmother**. A targeted retrieval of the **25 November** original user discussion instead attributes the *proposed inexpensive Rossi revolver to Heather herself*. No user correction to a grandmother owner was recovered.

- **[D/P]** Heather (Saafiya's mother) "would have had something like" an inexpensive Rossi .38 Special. Treat this as **Heather's proposed personal possession**, not a confirmed on-screen item. **The grandmother attribution was an assistant-side consolidation mistake**, and should not be repeated as canon.
- **[L]** Saafiya's **engraved Beretta 84FS Cheetah (.380 ACP)** is treated in the older story bible as an heirloom from her father **Faisal/Farrell**. The author has not said the Rossi replaces this.
- **[D/P]** Saafiya also has a *separate, custom personal revolver* concept: **Smith & Wesson Model 640 (.357 Magnum)**, short barrel, concealed-hammer appearance, dark/black finish with ornate gold scrollwork, diamond-checkered wood grips and **"Saafiya"** script. The user specified its identity and decoration on **25 November**, after a more generic revolver/gift exploration on 24 November. This is **not Heather's inexpensive Rossi**.
- **[D/O]** How Saafiya obtains the custom revolver was expressly left open: potential gift, own purchase/commission, or another interpersonal route. An earlier assistant proposed links with the older alternative-styled acquaintance; **do not select that as a fact**. An early idea for a simple Taurus Model 85/.38 gift is a **superseded or competing exploratory identification**, not the same final specified design.
- **[A/O]** The poolside anime-comic art shows a black/gold engraved Smith & Wesson-style revolver in Saafiya's **right** hand. Art details should not be promoted into an approved overall life timeline or real-world prop-handling instructions.
- **[O]** Full firearm inventory and the place of each object in each playable period are not settled by these character and illustration discussions.

**Continuity correction priority:** Retain the older archived wording as historical notes if required, but annotate and correct all *active* summaries stating "maternal grandmother's Rossi".

## 2. Heather's earlier romantic history and motivations

This previously missing part of the mother's biography was described in a **25 November** user discussion:

- **[D/P]** Before her relationship with **Faisal/Farrell**, Heather had a **first boyfriend**, a strong, disciplined **Coloured man** with prior voluntary service in South Africa's standing army. He was associated with carrying a **Walther PPK** as a personal possession.
- **[D/P]** He became involved in a **violent confrontation / shooting incident** and **died from his injuries on the way to hospital**. Whether his own participation in the confrontation was justified, reckless, provoked, or some combination **remains deliberately ambiguous**. Do not assign motive, guilt or the legal outcome without a director decision.
- **[D/P]** His death may have prompted Heather to seek police service, partly to make practical change and partly from unresolved grief or **revenge-versus-justice** motivations. Later K9-related work is a possible extension, not a fixed record of dates or duties.
- **[I/O]** An assistant previously suggested approximate **1982–84** entry and a generational echo between Heather's retaliatory impulses and Saafiya's later conduct. These are *interpretations to evaluate*, not director-locked chronology or moral diagnosis.
- **[D]** Heather's later choices—sometimes strict or abrasive, yet also pragmatic, caring and willing to make real sacrifices for her daughter's opportunities—should not be reduced to a single revenge origin or moral label.
- **[O]** First boyfriend's name and age, time/place of the incident, legal context, his relationship to Heather's later family, and when Saafiya learns of him remain open.

## 3. Farsha school lavatory: the full event and institutional aftermath

The earlier archive said only that Farsha was injured and the school response was undetermined. An original **26 November** user account provides much more detail for the **working scene**, although the final screenplay and medical outcome are still open.

1. **[D]** Saafiya draws/lures Farsha into a **school lavatory**, watches through a **small mirror**, and confronts her about disturbing behaviour.
2. **[D]** Saafiya hits Farsha **once in the face**. Farsha recoils, strikes the **mirror or wall**, briefly loses consciousness and falls face-first. Bleeding/bruising develops around her eye/face; a **concussion is suspected**, not medically confirmed. The scene is alarming, not a neat victory moment.
3. **[D]** After roughly **30–60 minutes**, a **female matric prefect** calls Saafiya to the school office. Saafiya is questioned, then brought to see the injured Farsha in the **sickbay**.
4. **[D]** **Aunt Isadora**—Saafiya's guardian/caretaker at the Cape Town school—is summoned to the **principal's office**. She remains composed, thanks the school staff, **accepts responsibility**, and asks to take Saafiya home/release her for the day. She neither shouts, strikes, nor publicly shames Saafiya.
5. **[D]** Isadora takes Saafiya to **McDonald's for a "second breakfast"**. Saafiya is still tense and barely eats; Isadora suggests **a bran muffin and coffee**. This quiet civilian scene follows the difficult school meeting rather than functioning as a reward for violence.
6. **[D]** Outside McDonald's, Saafiya notices another **schoolgirl absent from school**, subsequently developed as **Sarah**. Sarah is with friends awaiting a Dark Mountain online acquaintance whom they plan to meet face to face; this is their first **sighting/recognition**, not necessarily the beginning of their friendship. The sustained connection grows later at school, especially in **art class**.
7. **[O]** The exact degree of Farsha's injury, recovery and later behaviour, the school's official disciplinary record, the scene's final dialogue, and the exact moment the two girls first speak remain unresolved. **Do not erase the prefect, sickbay, Isadora's response or McDonald's link** merely because the scene still needs scripting.

**Distinctive causal chain:** encounter -> unexpected serious harm -> school's institutional response -> aunt's calm guardianship -> subdued second breakfast -> sighting of Sarah -> later friendship. This makes Sarah's entry a natural part of Saafiya's life after a difficult day, rather than an isolated coincidence inserted merely for atmosphere.

Those mechanical specifications are not required for story-level continuity.

- **[A]** The image titled *Saafiya’s Midnight Poolside Showdown* visually demonstrates the custom revolver direction; it **does not** prove this episode occurs or that it predates receipt of the revolver.
