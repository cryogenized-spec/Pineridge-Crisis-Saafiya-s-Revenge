## Working principles

- **Preserve, attribute and label:** distinguish established earlier lore, new director direction, proposed scenes, assistant interpretations, personal historical recollection and **verified implementation**.
- **Keep contradictions visible:** Saafiya/Fay versus Sophia/Sofia; KZN origin versus Cape Town legacy; Sarah's circle versus Dark Mountain; C/Home & More versus Saafiya's storyline; Copperlane versus KZN first chapter.
- **Do not fictionalise private historical individuals by default:** Bella/Paladona, Ronaldo and other Dark Mountain members are sources of inspiration, not automatically characters in the game.
- **No retrofit without a decision:** changing a lore file does not modify game code, assets, story chapters, approved designs or the older repository.
- **Protect future authorship:** if a topic is still vague, describe it as a *fragment* rather than generating a tidy but invented explanation.
- **Keep paranormal material appropriately scoped:** Ambrosia may include real extraordinary gifts **in the fiction**. Do not convert recollected real-life experiences into claims of independent scientific verification.
- **Use the character before the plot:** her daily competence, care, standards, weaknesses, kindness, independence, relationships and imperfect growth should not disappear behind combat or gothic atmosphere.

## Future updates

Add new ideas to the relevant topical document, timestamp significant decisions in the register and keep the preserved snapshots intact. If a continuous screenplay/timeline is later approved, build it *from* these records without deleting the alternate fragments.
