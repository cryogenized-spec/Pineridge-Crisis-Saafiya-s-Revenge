# Sixth-pass lore recovery — school micro-details, dialogue fragments and first game scenario

**Date:** 28 November 2026. **Status:** additive source review; *not* a unified chronology or claim that the content is implemented in the current Godot game. This sweep compared the first five recovery passes, original Hero-shooter documentation, a preserved **PINE RIDGE AT DAWN — OVERHEAD PROOF OF CONCEPT** README in the Library, older character-art metadata, and recoverable director conversations of 22–26 November.

Labels: **[D]** director-described idea or preference; **[P]** possible scene or undecided narrative fact; **[L]** existing legacy project/story documentation; **[V]** visual concept or illustration only; **[I]** assistant idea *not* adopted by the director; **[H]** user recollection, distinct from fictional facts; **[O]** unresolved. Some details can carry multiple labels; a historically built prototype does not establish the final story.

## A. Farsha: the childhood doll detail, twin and routine

**The earlier records describe her dolls and a possible lost twin, but omit the more specific emotional texture.**

- **[D/P]** Farsha once had a warm, imaginative Afrikaans-speaking home. As her parents became emotionally less available, she would carefully tend **two or three Barbie dolls**, play out elaborate everyday and fantasy lives, and wonder what adulthood would bring. The dolls are not merely generic evidence that she was lonely: they are a way she could nurture, arrange and inhabit an alternate social world.
- **[D/P]** In the 25 November working discussion, a **twin dying during infancy** was described as a possible formative history. Earlier summaries preserved only the vaguer *"lost twin"*. Retain *infancy* as part of that **version**, while the existence, nature and relevance of the loss remain **unfinalised**. Do not infer that Farsha consciously remembers a sibling who died as an infant.
- **[D/P]** Farsha remains **short or relatively small in stature** as another proposed detail. No adult height or medical explanation has been established.
- **[D]** Cruel school attention to her **Transvaal Afrikaans accent** creates a specific contrast: she avoids speaking in the English-medium environment even as her **written English improves** through messaging. Her silence is social camouflage, not Sarah's chosen contemplative quietness.
- **[D/P]** Her MXit introduction follows a recognisably ordinary path: library boys, an improvised chess set on a roll-up board with pieces in an ice-cream container, older Nokia/GPRS chatter, a borrowed phone from her mother, and later potentially her own Nokia from her father. She explores fantasy communities **late at night**, with one earlier example placing her first Dark Mountain observation **around 23:00**. This is a scene-time possibility, not an unchangeable first-login timestamp.
- **[D/P]** She initially watches how others type and interact before projecting a confident written character voice. The later possible school obsession with Saafiya should not erase Farsha's separate creative/online accomplishments.

**Keep clear:** Ambrosia was an offline friend circle that later took to MXit; Farsha's group originated online. Their overlapping schools do not establish that their clans were identical.

- **[D/P]** The old **grey Renault 5** journey is quiet and emotionally restrained, with **two suitcases and a further bag supplied by Heather**. An old cassette of **Sandra's "(I'll Never Be) Maria Magdalena"** and a **Princeton cigarette in the door pocket** are unusually concrete period details; do not swap them for generic melancholy music or a generic cigarette without an explicit rewrite.
- **[D/P]** The director supplied advice along the lines of **"Your heart will guide you"** and **"Don't do yourself injustice."** The exact final dialogue and speaker timing remain open, but these phrasings carry Heather's pragmatic concern and faith in Saafiya's agency. Another variant says **"follow your heart"**.
- **[P/I]** A **fragile glass ball** representing the daughter's future / personal integrity arose within that conversation. Keep it as a *metaphor under development*, with the final spoken wording unapproved.
- **[O]** The earlier journey was called **about two hours**; a literal KwaZulu-Natal-to-Cape-Town road trip takes much longer. Do not manufacture a route fix: it could represent a shorter leg, an earlier origin plan or an abandoned duration.
- **[O]** When Heather's first boyfriend's death, subsequent policing/K9 history and her daughter’s later relocation are integrated, the emotional link should be *earned*. Nothing yet establishes that Heather told Saafiya about that earlier boyfriend during this drive.

## C. Original Pine Ridge at Dawn scenario — historical gameplay reference, not final screenplay

A **Library file titled \`README(20261122-104832).md\`** documents the earlier **PINE RIDGE AT DAWN — OVERHEAD PROOF OF CONCEPT**. This source relates an early **HTML/JavaScript side-view scene**, a standalone **Python/Tkinter GTA-2-like overhead adaptation**, and later Godot work. The original README is *implementation documentation*, not a confirmed narrative synopsis.

The **original prototype's dramatic spine** is more specific than the phrase *"taxi ambush"* used in the lore:

1. **[L/V]** Saafiya appears **at dawn, outside a fictional mall** in a relatively long road environment. An earlier narrative variant says she has left a **car near a barricaded approach** and is attempting to reach **Mr Fahim inside the mall**. Do not assume that this is already reconciled with the later Fayeem/Copperlane plot.
2. **[L]** A mixed crowd of civilian passers-by and threatening looter characters occupies the street. Some individuals flee or avoid violence; others confront the protagonist. **Civilians and aggressors are deliberately distinct categories**, not a demographic-based enemy classification.
3. **[L]** Near the **taxi rank**, a **white minibus taxi** pulls up, stops, **three people emerge**, and it leaves. Another director-described map revision envisioned a **single-lane taxi pull-in at roughly 45 degrees with a planted island**. Those are **game-map design variants**, not a real tactical layout or a settled geographic location.
4. **[L]** A prominent boss-character confrontation occurs toward the road's eastern end, followed by an **east-end exit objective**. The original prototype included dramatic action and a recoverable fictional rifle item; do not extrapolate an armed character's identity, motives or relation to Russian without a screenplay decision.
5. **[L]** The retained original archetypes include ages **22, 28 and 35**, wearing ordinary **track jacket / checked overshirt / work jacket** variants, with varied street props. These are **visual/roster presets**, not grounds to assign combatant status by age, clothing, language or ethnicity.
6. **[D/L]** The director also asked for **numerous genuine noncombatants**, convincing crowd reaction and characters with individually distinct behaviours, rather than a street populated only by enemies. A later request referenced **ten distinct civilians**. These are city-crowd storytelling/design requirements, not ten confirmed, named future characters.
7. **[L]** Short South African conversational alerts were used, including **"Ubani?"** and **"Qaphela!"**. These are *source-game dialogue cues*; their speakers, dialect, context and placement require story and language review before being treated as screenplay lines.
8. **[L/O]** The old portable prototype represents an **8-round Beretta**; later story canon fixes an **inherited Beretta 84FS, .380 ACP** with different profile. Avoid quietly treating the old ammo UI as a newly approved firearm-identity retcon.

**Provenance / implementation boundaries:**
- This earlier overhead version was a **standalone Tkinter prototype**, *not* the later native **Godot** project. Its README says it preserved original sprite art/sound for reference and generated new overhead sprites; it did **not** implement full cinematic dialogue, indoor exploration or every prior gameplay system.
- Later native Godot documentation confirms more chapters such as **Upper Pineridge** and **House One**, inventory, crowd systems and approved imported character atlases. **Do not describe the Tkinter stage as a current verified Godot scene without inspecting the corresponding Godot project.**
- Original source / clue: Library document **\`README(20261122-104832).md\`**. No copy of that Library file or its ZIP was made by this sixth-pass documentation update.

## F. Historical Dark Mountain context worth keeping as texture, not as literal chapter history

- **[H]** The director recalls older chat users with decades of online experience; **MSN, Yahoo, forums, existing contacts and expressive text emoticons** persisted into MXit's roleplay world. Both older adults and young participants occupied the same virtual social space.
- **[H]** This overlapping generation also meant that a **roleplayed reputation sometimes survived the boundaries of a session**. Other people reacted to the name, typing style, role, reputation and established friendships as a lasting part of the person's online identity. Emotional closeness and roleplay could become entangled.
- **[D]** In *the fiction*, some Ambrosia practitioners truly have unusual abilities, some have lesser or no gifts, while **Saafiya's dexterity, speed, situational judgment and independence** remain central. Sarah's status as the **Oracle** is not an assertion that she leads the coven or possesses its greatest powers.
- **[O]** How literal spirituality, costume/LARP, online clan identities and private friendships intersect in **Saafiya's particular path** is unfinished. Preserve that uncertainty. Do not back-project real users' reported paranormal experiences into confirmed historical proof.

## G. Why these recovered fragments matter

The new details have three different narrative functions:

- **Character intimacy**: the dolls, school shame, Heather's advice, quiet car and Sarah's everyday objects offer a life between headline plot points.
- **Scene continuity**: the school/Isadora/McDonald's meeting, separated from later tabletop/Ambrosia, and the historical dawn/taxi version provide possible chapter structures with explicit transitions.
- **Source integrity**: distinguishes the early side-scrolling HTML, the overhead Tkinter prototype, the Godot build and Library illustrations. The same character name across them does not guarantee the same narrative continuity.

**Do not treat this audit as a new canon vote.** It identifies details missing from the first five notes and collects them with the appropriate source/version flags. See the [continuity register](continuity-register.md) for unresolved decisions.
