# Sarah, Ambrosia, Dark Mountain — relationship and worldbuilding fragments

**Working archive, 28 November 2026.** Tracks direct ideas from discussions, assistant suggestions and firsthand historical inspiration separately. See [unified Saafiya entry](saafiya-unified-notes.md) and [continuity register](continuity-register.md).

**Labels:** **[D]** director-stated direction, **[P]** proposed scene/interpretation, **[O]** undecided, **[H]** participant's real-world recollection (source inspiration, **not** proof a fictional event happened), **[I]** assistant interpretation not yet ratified.

## Sarah — the girl behind "the Oracle"

- **[D]** English-speaking, Irish-family background in the Cape Town school environment. Dark straight or straightened hair, sharp/irregular fringe, distinctive green eyes, eyeliner and other carefully considered makeup, with an ornate inherited hairpin. A talented illustrator and a reader of fantasy/gothic lore; interested in heavier alternative music, costume, narrative and myth.
- **[D]** Highly attentive, deliberate with eye movements, sparing with speech, comfortable with silence. Perceived as unusually calm, aware or even goddess-like by admirers; she **does not insist on reverence**. She accepts **"Oracle"** respectfully as a title within her circle while remaining comfortable being ordinary, poor or unranked.
- **[D]** Sees herself as a *provisional leader or custodian* responsible for caring for people, standards and the group's future rather than possessing a throne. She is neither the founder nor the most senior member. A rough third/fourth position in a flexible informal hierarchy is one possibility; **exact ranking [O]**.
- **[D]** She moves among multiple school friendship circles like a butterfly, has a strong sense of empathy, and is able to choose Saafiya's company for its own sake rather than only out of pity or a wish to improve her.
- **[D]** Her mother has attempted suicide and has patterns of alcohol misuse. Sarah maintains her mother's dignity, resists shouting at her or gossiping to her grandmother, but may carry unspoken distress. Respect and discretion are not proof that the household is safe or that asking for responsible adult help would be disloyal.
- **[D/P]** A secluded school drawing place is Sarah's refuge; she secretly smokes there, a flawed coping habit rather than a glamorous credential.
- **[D]** Sarah's gift is especially judgment, patient listening, artistic skill, imaginative breadth and social caretaking. She does not need to be the coven's strongest paranormal practitioner to warrant the title Oracle.
- **[I]** People seek her counsel so regularly that her role risks hiding her own needs. Saafiya's uncomplicated friendship eventually allows Sarah to drop the Oracle persona without feeling diminished.

## First sightings, art and the bond

- **[D]** An early meeting or sighting is set outside **McDonald's** while Saafiya is with **Aunt Isadora** after a school incident; Sarah wears denim and a Victorian-inspired corset-like top, ornate hairpin, and is with others awaiting an online acquaintance from MXit's Dark Mountain. Whether the girls actually talk there is [O].
- **[D/P]** Sarah later takes notice of Saafiya's fantasy drawings in an art classroom. Saafiya may be technically weaker at anatomy/perspective yet personally invested in her creations. Sarah approaches, offers an occasional adjustment, later asks the teacher to move closer and develops a friendship through listening and companionship.
- **[D]** The art teacher is a kindly, open-minded woman whose room may display Celtic script, knots, seasonal imagery and art or folklore motifs. This does not alone define her religious affiliation. Saafiya may choose art partly because of the teacher and the environment.
- **[D/P]** Quieter first friends are a separate group of girls who initially welcome Saafiya; they should not vanish as disposable placeholders once Sarah appears. Their individual interests may include anime, collecting, stories, music, schoolwork or art.
- **[D]** Sarah introduces clothing, makeup, music, food and imaginative aesthetics indirectly. She may give a modest starter collection (choker/pendant, lace or other garments, basic makeup) and occasionally improve small details in Saafiya's pictures. She does **not** prescribe popularity, a partner or a completed personality.
- **[D/P]** A dramatic but intimate **full-length Victorian mirror reveal**: Sarah's mirror has been kept covered and wrapped. Saafiya consents to being briefly blindfolded while Sarah helps her adopt a gothic presentation, then studies her reflection in silence. A possible line/idea is "If you don't like what you see, you can become something else"; its healthier interpretation is **choice and self-expression**, not self-hatred or erasure. Exact dialogue [O].
- **[D]** Sarah then steps into the background for several days. Saafiya receives appreciative notice from some girls and boys, finds ways to negotiate teachers and dress codes, and makes the look her own without requiring Sarah's approval.
- **[D]** Saafiya actively goes looking for Sarah, finds her at the secluded sketching/smoking spot, and renews the friendship. The choice to seek *Sarah herself*, not the Oracle or social patron, strongly affects Sarah.
- **[D/P]** Sarah responds by returning to a fuller version of her own goth style, straightening her hair and retrieving long-neglected pieces. In a quiet moment she takes Saafiya's hand and holds it slightly too long. **Director clarification: this can establish a girl-to-girl understanding / bond**; romance is neither mandated nor excluded. Keep it subtle and unresolved.
- **[I]** Their bond becomes reciprocal: Sarah originally invites discovery; Saafiya later invigorates Sarah's capacity to enjoy her own interests, rather than merely shepherding others.

## Ambrosia — offline life before Dark Mountain

- **[D]** **Ambrosia** begins as a real-world gathering of existing friends. It is **not founded as an MXit Dark Mountain clan**. Only later does it extend its activities and existing relationships into the Mountain because inexpensive mobile messaging makes it practical.
- **[D]** Earlier "Victoria's Boudoir" was floated as a name; Ambrosia is preferred. Its original social shape includes girls reading and swapping fantasy/gothic books, trying makeup, listening to music, drawing, sharing stories and having intimate but ordinary conversations. It can later grow into a mixed-age creative/coven network; precise membership boundaries remain open.
- **[D/P]** A natural hobby progression: friends around a table with pencils, dice and character sheets -> collaborative fantasy adventure -> informal acting out of characters, handmade props and costumes -> small private live-action events -> contact with broader Cape Town LARP and art/drama circles -> online text roleplay.
- **[D]** Sarah can be a respected younger member while older members (some university students, working adults, perhaps people in their 30s–50s) have existing tabletop, MSN, forum and LARP histories. These adults are peers/mentors in creative activities, not universal owners of the younger members' decisions.
- **[D/I]** Ambrosia may have overlapping interests, *not* rigid castes: (1) artistic/roleplay/social contributors, (2) folklore/ritual/spiritual explorers, and (3) long-practising senior members within the fictional world who have unusual powers. Some individuals move among all three; equally valued members may have no paranormal gifts.
- **[D]** A number of its fictional practitioners **really possess supernatural gifts in the story's world**. Suggested varieties include remote perception, insight into personal circumstances, extraordinary healing and exceptional rarities such as fire/heat manifestations. Many members do not possess such abilities; the group is not predominantly a supernatural combat academy.
- **[O]** How any gift works, how reliably it operates, how it is learned, tested, limited or ethically governed, and how many people possess it remain unsettled narrative mechanics. **No claim about real-world proof of psychic powers follows from these fictional design choices.**
- **[D/I]** "Possession of a gift does not automatically confer wisdom" is a useful working principle. Members can disagree over privacy, ethics, whether spiritual practice is appropriate, and how much to reveal to outsiders.
- **[D]** Gothic/alternative music may expand into Celtic and modern folk/pagan-flavoured music, folklore, spirituality and questions about the meaning of life. Cultural exploration does not imply one fixed religious belief, nor does a pixie nickname imply paganism.

## The tabletop-to-LARP gateway

- **[D]** Saafiya is not initially a hardened computer gamer or fantasy scholar. A childhood console, simple collectibles and home drawings give fragments; Sarah and friends introduce the *collaborative* mechanics of actual tabletop roleplay.
- **[D/P]** First private session could be a Saturday with four or five friends, snacks, dice, notebooks, hand-drawn maps and a patient explanation of character attributes and a game master. She may play an elf, mage, ranger/archer, healer or something unlike her normal social self.
- **[I]** Team puzzles teach that a forceful personality is not automatically the most effective decision-maker. Learning flexibility, improvisation and respect for the less conspicuous player's idea may gradually reinforce her mediation skills.
- **[D/P]** A costume emerges from illustrations, a handmade accessory or a garden performance. Saafiya is capable of organising people and practical resources while Sarah excels at imagery and dramatic craft. Imperfect attempts to stay in character can be charming; avoid framing the hobby as permanently solemn ritual.
- **[D]** Roleplayed identity may continue beyond a session via habits, reputations, writing and friend networks; identity/play boundaries can blur without requiring loss of reality-testing. Character bleed and online-persona persistence are thematic possibilities, not diagnoses.

## Dark Mountain — real historical inspiration, clearly distinguished

The following are the director's **firsthand recollections** of the real MXit Dark Mountain community, not automatic facts about the fictional characters. Do not rewrite them as a universal, fully verified history without independent material.

- **[H]** Community in the mid/late-2000s MXit Kingdom milieu, combining competitive text battle roleplay, crafted character personas, clans, alliances, loyalty, collaborative scenes, social performance and close friendships. The online relationships could be extremely meaningful and sometimes messy, including rapidly changing romances.
- **[H]** Earlier culture placed weight on reputation, commitment, taboo/boundaries and standards; some later users brought different social norms and successfully organised social events, even as older members worried about rivalry, turbulence or deterioration.
- **[H]** Several age cohorts coexisted: adolescents and young adults alongside people in their forties or fifties. Some older participants already knew dial-up-era MSN, Yahoo, chat emoticons, forums and tabletop/LARP communities, and brought existing friendship networks into new chat venues.
- **[H/O]** Recalled chat-room maximum may have begun at **seven** then increased to **ten**; specific phases and ceilings need independent historical checking. Never turn a fluctuating recollection into a fixed feature requirement.
- **[H]** A prominent real female participant recalled as **Bella**, with a Spanish-style nickname remembered as **Paladona**, was accomplished in fighting/text roleplay, clan contribution and maintaining community standards. She had earned standing in powerful groups including **the Covenant**; name/spelling and exact affiliation chronology may need archival verification.
- **[H]** **Ronaldo**, remembered as the formidable leader of the Covenant and a significant roleplayer, was close to or involved with Bella; other clan names and a Dragon-clan connection arise in recollection, with the exact relationships not all settled.
- **[H]** These people are historical inspirations for *the pattern* of an influential, competent, community-minded woman. They are **not literally Sarah or Saafiya**; do not import their private lives or identities into fiction automatically.
- **[H]** The user played a vampire-lord persona and recruited participants based on writing style, affect and interpersonal energy, becoming close to some. The persona acquired a lasting reputation. Roleplaying, romantic attachments and real spiritual/psychic explorations could overlap for participants.
- **[H]** The user reports experiences interpreted as remote perception / clairvoyance and tests with others. Preserve as *recollections about personal experience* when needed; do not label them scientifically confirmed events. A story may independently decide that fictional supernatural abilities are real.
- **[H]** A **2018 revival/jumpstart** of Dark Mountain was attempted and the user recalls being among its driving participants. Public recollections and surviving revival material may supplement these memories; whether each detail appears in archives is a separate question.

## Lowercase handle and social reputation

- **[D]** Saafiya's initial name could be **pixie**, perhaps with numbers if unavailable. A later extension might become **moonlitpixie** or **moonlightpixie**. Lowercase is preferable for the visual realism of the era. No final exact handle yet.
- **[D]** A drawing of a pixie, an art-class conversation, a teacher's Celtic imagery or Sarah's whimsical compliment can be competing/or complementary origin fragments. Do not select one solely because it sounds literary.
- **[D/I]** Over time the handle gains meaning from merit, contributions and personal relationships. Her reputation can become formidable while still being compatible with fairness, kindness to strangers and the benefit of the doubt.

## Scene seed: the courtyard return (not final screenplay)

Sarah has stood aside after helping Saafiya find a style she likes. Others have noticed Saafiya; some approve, others are baffled. Saafiya persists. Several days later she locates Sarah behind the art rooms with a sketchbook and cigarette. She approaches without seeking a lesson, a gift or an introduction—simply because Sarah has been absent. Sarah realises the connection has become mutual. A pause, a held hand, an ordinary exchange and eventual departure accomplish more than an explicit speech. Later Sarah brings out her old hairpin and fuller gothic wardrobe.

This is an **outline, not a locked shot list, nor proof of a romance**.

## Open relationships and design questions

- Who founded Ambrosia, what was the age mix at first founding, and who are the older members?
- Is its mystical side already active when Saafiya enters, or revealed gradually after months of tabletop/LARP socialising?
- Does "the Oracle" refer only to Sarah's social judgment or also a minor fictional ability?
- Does Sarah's mother or grandmother know the full extent of her second life?
- What were the dates of the McDonald's sighting, the art-class friendship, mirror reveal, courtyard return and first tabletop session?
- What precise cosmetics, clothing and art items are given or loaned? Which has lasting significance?
- Is Ambrosia all-female in its early private form, then mixed, or do men remain visitors? What protections and group boundaries exist given minors and older adults?
- Do Saafiya and Sarah remain lifelong close friends, drift apart, or rejoin during the adult crisis?
- When does the offline group discover MXit's Mountain, and where do the Covenant/Farsha rivalries intersect if fictionalised at all?
- Is Saafiya ever spiritually gifted, or does she remain largely human and practical while learning basic forms of attention and concentration?
