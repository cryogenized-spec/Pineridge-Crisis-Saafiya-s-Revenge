# Saafiya / Sophia / Sofia — consolidated character and story bible

**Working consolidation — 28 November 2026.** This is an additive *working record*, not a compulsory retcon of scripts or art. Sources: discussions through 28 November 2026 and the archived Hero-shooter Markdown files in [source-hero-shooter](source-hero-shooter/). It is intentionally possible for entries here to contradict one another.

**Status:** **[L]** explicitly recorded legacy Hero-shooter continuity; **[D]** recent director-described character/world direction; **[P]** exploratory possibility or scene treatment; **[O]** unresolved/contradictory; **[I]** interpretation offered during discussion, not yet a director-approved fact. A detail can be [L] and [O] if a later proposal may supersede it. **Never quietly promote [P]/[I] into canon.**

## Identity and story framework

- **[L/O]** The existing story calls her **Saafiya**, familiar shorthand **Fay**; source code and art have also used Sophia/Sofia. Recent discussion freely calls her **Sophia**, sometimes **Saafiya**. A proposed Sofia birth name / Sophia school name has not been settled; *Saafiya* is not simply another transliteration of the same Greek name. Do not rename assets or assert a legal identity until resolved.
- **[L/O]** One character history describes a woman raised in a coloured Muslim family in **Cape Town**, with an Afrikaans-speaking Cape Flats mother and a more polished, financially secure father. A newer origin prefers **KwaZulu-Natal**, with a traumatic loss followed by an extended **Cape Town high-school period** and eventual Gauteng storylines. These versions remain side by side.
- **[D]** The late-2000s school years, particularly Grades 10–12, are important to her evolving identity, friendships, aesthetics and entry into fantasy communities. Matric would occur after years of this gradual development. Exact year, age, school and birthdate are open.
- **[L]** Adult Saafiya is self-contained, practically capable, difficult to intimidate and protective of her private life, with a life outside the central conflict.
- **[D]** Avoid a simple transformation from a supposedly unsophisticated person into a sophisticated one: she already has standards, competence, self-respect and creativity; Cape Town allows her to discover *different forms* of excellence and social strength.

## Appearance, possessions and visual continuity

- **[L/D]** Approved adult gothic direction: medium-tan complexion; expressive eyes, pronounced eyeliner and dark lipstick; beauty mark; dark, voluminous hair with burgundy/purple accents; hoop earrings/piercings; sunglasses on her head; choker/crescent pendant; lace or fishnet sleeves; corset/layered clothing; black/plum/silver palette, belts or chains, tall buckled boots. This is a recurring visual language, not a fixed uniform for every chapter.
- **[D/P]** School-era goth emerges slowly through friendships, music, makeup, clothing gifts and experiment, not as an instant makeover that erases her old personality. Sarah's high-Victorian gothic styling can be richer or more ceremonial; Saafiya pares some elements down to suit her own tastes, practical habits, hair and school dress rules.
- **[L]** The inherited engraved **Beretta 84FS Cheetah, .380 ACP**, with three spare magazines, is the final pistol designation recorded in the older canon; old 7.65 mm / Model 82 / single-stack variants are superseded *as weapon-identification lore*, although older rendered assets may retain provisional proportions. It matters because of its paternal inheritance. **No real-world mechanical or usage instructions are intended.**
- **[L]** She loves her **black Ford Mustang**, with enthusiastic driving a personal expression of freedom. The current game additionally describes a black **1967 Mustang**; exact historical model can be checked for continuity.
- **[D/P]** A single elongated **black/grey dragon- or bat-like wing** tattoo on the left upper back/shoulder blade, with spaced lowercase vertical **v i t a l u s** at the spine, is a later proposed visual story beat; usually concealed, acquired through an artist's offer to use her as a living canvas, with further circumstances unresolved. The collar's possible later relationship meaning is also unresolved.
- **[O]** Prior prototype artwork elsewhere includes another blonde-ponytail protagonist **C**. Do not collapse the separate C and Saafiya identities or import C's characteristics into Saafiya without an explicit story decision. Earlier Saafiya/Sophia sprite versions can remain archival alternatives.

## Family and earlier life — retained legacy and newer development

- **[D]** Her father, born **Faisal Rasool**, later used **Farrell** as a chosen identity. Exact legal name remains open. He is broad/stocky, measured, formally mannered, increasingly questioning of inherited religious conventions, and capable of practical managerial/logistical improvement. His influence includes accountability, initiative and evidence-sensitive reasoning. Do not reduce any religion or ethnic community to a caricature.
- **[D/P]** Farrell's professional arc may involve warehousing, procurement, operations, supply chain, workflow discipline and gradual advancement through useful incremental improvements. A possible later assassination following his exposure of procurement wrongdoing is *proposed*, not a settled solved murder.
- **[D/P]** Her mother is Afrikaans-speaking, from a Cape Flats background, possibly with police/K9 experience and a harsh disciplinary temperament. Family tension and the father's intervening influence may shape Saafiya's independence. The mother remains an individual, not a representative stereotype.
- **[D/P]** After her father's death, grief and mounting school conflict may help motivate a carefully planned transfer to Cape Town. The mother discusses arrangements over several days, pays school and accommodation fees, perhaps two terms in advance.
- **[D]** Aunt **Isadora** (name used in later discussions; compare older unnamed-aunt notes) is a capable, older businesswoman with Victorian/Edwardian-style guest accommodation, separate cottages and a communal pool. She prepares a clean white cottage, bed, desk and storage to receive Saafiya. Possible suburbs **Tamboerskloof** or **Oranjezicht**; actual school, aunt's exact name/surname and location remain open.
- **[P]** An ex-office Windows XP desktop or comparable used PC may be obtained for schoolwork and private leisure. It offers research, music, messaging and modest early games, not instant advanced PC-game expertise.
- **[P]** Earlier childhood may have included a Nintendo/NES-like console later sold or taken away by her mother, producing a lingering resentment. Childhood Tazos/trading cards, collectables, character sketches and fantasy media are possible modest precursors; games such as Yu-Gi-Oh! and Pokémon belong to the period but her precise collection is not fixed.
- **[D/P]** She enjoys drawing for its own sake, including fairies, fantasy figures, clothing and ornament, while often struggling with perspective/three-dimensional anatomy. She is *not* effortlessly gifted at everything. Artistic satisfaction is separate from professional skill.

## School temperament and intellectual development

- **[D]** Saafiya is orderly rather than messy, particular about her standards, initially reserved and selective. She values civility, considered speech, fairness, self-possession, restraint and meaningful intellectual exchange.
- **[D]** Initially she may be somewhat hotheaded and physically assertive. In a less confrontational school environment she can briefly feel like a stronger, bigger fish because others do not compete through physical intimidation. Experience reveals that self-restraint, creativity, reasoning and quiet expertise are other kinds of strength.
- **[D]** She should learn not to mistake disagreement for hostility, to give the benefit of the doubt, to wait for evidence, to change her mind without humiliation, and to apply the same standards to herself. This is learned imperfectly over time, with occasional lapses, not an immediate saintly conversion.
- **[D/I]** Mediation develops through group participation, competitive games, social practice, and the willingness to recognise *her own* part in escalating conflict. Being a capable mediator does not mean never facing opposition; it means responding more selectively and fairly.
- **[D/P]** Early school friends may be quieter academically or creatively inclined girls who welcome a newcomer. They must have lives beyond serving as a bridge to Sarah; comics, anime, trading cards, schoolwork, music and novels are possibilities, not a gendered rule.
- **[P]** Art is chosen partly because she enjoys an open-minded female teacher and the classroom atmosphere, not solely because she is an elite draughtswoman. The teacher's art may include Celtic knots, seasonal cycles or folklore motifs without any fixed religious identity.

## Sarah — essential relationship

- **[D]** Sarah, an English-speaking girl from an Irish-family background, has black/near-black hair with a distinctive fringe, green eyes, dark makeup, an old ornate hairpin, a highly developed aptitude for drawing, fantasy/gothic reading and alternative music. She is observant, composed and capable of moving among several groups rather than defining herself through a single clique.
- **[D]** Earlier planned sighting: Saafiya and Aunt Isadora encounter Sarah outside a McDonald's, where Sarah and acquaintances are waiting for somebody connected to Dark Mountain. Do not replace this with the later art-class friendship: first sighting, first recognition and genuine friendship may be different events.
- **[D/P]** At school Sarah notices Saafiya's fantasy sketch and offers suggestions, eventually asking the teacher to sit/work beside her. The friendship grows through repeated voluntary company, listening, artistic guidance and mutual curiosity. Sarah is the considerably stronger artist, but does not wholly overwrite Saafiya's work.
- **[D]** Sarah helps Saafiya explore gothic expression indirectly through music, makeup, clothes, small gifts, food and imagination. Her aim is self-discovery, not producing a copy of herself or making Saafiya desirable to popular boys.
- **[D/P]** A defining Victorian mirror scene: Sarah covers/keeps a full-height ornate mirror, helps Saafiya dress and apply makeup, guides her toward it with her agreement and removes a blindfold so she can see a different possible presentation of herself. The significance is freedom to choose an identity, not a magically imposed makeover.
- **[D]** Sarah steps backstage afterward so Saafiya can make independent friendships, receive attention from girls and boys, decide her own style, and persist even against ordinary school discouragement. A modest 'starter pack' of clothes/accessories and selective small art touch-ups can remain part of the friendship.
- **[D/P]** After a few days of Sarah seeming withdrawn, Saafiya independently seeks her at a secluded school drawing spot where Sarah sometimes secretly smokes. The reunion is personally reinvigorating for Sarah. She revives her older, more elaborate gothic self-presentation and straightens her hair. She briefly holds Saafiya's hand longer than either expects: a bond of trust, shared understanding and girl-to-girl affection, **not automatic proof of romance**.
- **[D/I]** Reciprocity matters: Sarah first brings Saafiya into another world; Saafiya then reminds Sarah of how much she loved that world and seeks Sarah herself, rather than the revered Oracle. Sarah may be moved by this more than by admiration from others.

## Creative progression: art -> tabletop -> costume/LARP -> Dark Mountain

- **[D]** A modest home tabletop session among schoolfriends is the preferred first gateway. Dice, character sheets, invented scenarios and teamwork teach Saafiya rules, improvisation, imaginative problem-solving and collaboration.
- **[D/P]** She may play an elf, ranger/archer, sorceress, healer, mage or another role that need not reproduce her real-life assertiveness. Other players surprise her; a quiet participant's solution can succeed where headstrong confrontation does not.
- **[D/P]** Friends begin illustrating their characters, making props or costumes, improvising scenes in homes/gardens, then developing informal live-action roleplay. Saafiya contributes practical organisation and adaptability; Sarah brings experienced drawing, aesthetic design, research and dramatic imagination.
- **[D]** **Ambrosia originated as an offline circle of friends**, not as a Dark Mountain clan. Only later do members extend existing friendships and characters into MXit/Dark Mountain, assisted by inexpensive GPRS messaging. Preserve the offline-first sequence.
- **[D]** The first informal online handle is plausibly lowercase **pixie** or a numbered variant, later **moonlitpixie** or **moonlightpixie** (exact spelling undecided). A familiar pretty-sounding nickname need not denote occult allegiance. Reputation attaches to conduct and contribution over time.
- **[D]** Saafiya could eventually excel in Dark Mountain's text roleplay and community work, while Sarah remains influential within Ambrosia. Neither must dominate the other's domain.

## Ambrosia, Sarah's second life, and the occult

See [Sarah, Ambrosia and Dark Mountain](sarah-ambrosia-dark-mountain.md) for the full separate treatment.

- **[D]** Ambrosia spans reading, beauty/makeup, costume, tabletop games, artistic interests, fantasy, friendship and, for some members, folklore or spiritual practice. It may include older students and adults with experience predating MXit. A fictional coven can contain diverse levels of belief and competence.
- **[D]** Sarah is widely admired for composure, watchfulness and carefully directed attention. Called the **Oracle**, she acknowledges the title without insisting on it, and is as comfortable in an ordinary supporting role as in honour. She sees leadership as *temporary custodianship*, not ownership.
- **[D]** She is influential but **not the senior founder**, perhaps third or fourth in an informal hierarchy. Her standing reflects judgment, creative work, reliability and contribution, not a required supernatural ranking. "Younger flower" is a possible affectionate designation rather than a formal office.
- **[D]** Sarah lives with the difficulty of a mother who has attempted suicide and has problematic alcohol use. She chooses discretion and respect, refusing to turn this into gossip or public humiliation; it should not imply she must conceal real danger or never seek help from a trusted adult/grandmother.
- **[D]** Some senior Ambrosia practitioners may have genuine psychic or supernatural gifts **within the fiction**; others have only artistic, social, philosophical, ritual or roleplay interests. Gift types proposed include remote perception, insight into another person's circumstances, healing and very rare overt phenomena. Their narrative limits, repeatability and reliability are **[O]**. This is a fictional world-design choice, not an empirical statement about real-life powers.
- **[D]** Saafiya remains primarily fast, dexterous, observant, composed under pressure and able to think on her feet; any training or extraordinary augmentation stays elementary, understated and secondary to her natural competence.

## Personal attachments and adult romantic possibilities

- **[D]** Saafiya is selective, independent and not moved by formulaic courtship, status, conspicuous wealth or someone making her their sole obsession. She prefers a self-possessed, sincerely unconventional man with an independent life and convictions; friendship and mutual respect can precede romance by a long time.
- **[P/O]** A male Dark Mountain acquaintance first known online, possibly a rival or enduring friend, may develop into a later in-person relationship. Exact identity and place in the timeline are unsettled.
- **[D/P]** Another possibility involves an approximately **23-year-old** alternative-styled man (short black-dyed hair, green eyes, eyeliner, snakebite piercings) introduced at a cosplay/anime event **only after Saafiya has turned 18**. She later learns that two women friends engage in a discreet voluntary master/submissive relationship with him and needs time to think about consent, autonomy and trust. His connection to the eventual romance is **not settled**.
- **[P/O]** First-adult-intimacy concepts include a consensual power-dynamic experience or an awkward, character-breaking cosplay scenario. Neither is locked, nor are they necessarily the same partner. Avoid conflating fantasy roleplay with anyone's real-world consent.
- **[D]** Her adult romantic self can be more affectionate, initiative-taking, private and adventurous than her public reserve suggests; desire and intimacy remain distinct from promiscuity and from any obligatory role. All sexually intimate story arcs belong to clearly adult characters.

## Adult work and the crisis threads

- **[L]** Adult Saafiya manages in a fuel-station/restaurant setting, handles staff/customer responsibility and keeps her business life separate from fantasy interests. This is a concrete competence, not a cover for military training.
- **[L]** On-and-off partner **Fayeem** is technically gifted and formerly a Russian military lieutenant; his precise history is open. He concealed another relationship and an approximately two-year-old son. A Russian operative exploits the secret family as leverage to draw him into illicit interests. The exact operation, motives and outcome are unresolved.
- **[L]** In one developed Johannesburg opening, she is at Fayeem's family home when he vanishes abruptly, leaving food/cologne but taking possessions and a vehicle; an anonymous message leads her toward **125 Copperlane Ave**. She drives her Mustang in worsening weather to investigate. Keep the message's author and reliability unknown.
- **[D/P]** More recent development prefers a **KwaZulu-Natal 2021 crisis** as a narrative origin, followed later by Gauteng, organised crime and political corruption. She may receive a consequential lead in person from **Russian** about Fayeem, and enter the mall situation looking for him. Timing versus Copperlane and other openings has not been solved.
- **[D/P]** At the mall she may encounter **Ernest Meintjies ("Domino")**; his loyalties are unconfirmed. Exposure to earlier death does not make her first lethal act emotionally trivial. Her responses to danger, casualties, harm and choices should be context-sensitive.
- **[P/O]** A devastating branch **"But None of Us Will Win"** portrays her eventual succession to Russian's ruthless outlook and the destructive consequences; do not mistake a contemplated tragic branch for the principal ending.

## Game-world interlocks: do not merge blindly

- **[L]** The Hero-shooter **C / Sophia/Saafiya** sprite and protagonist mappings have overlapped in technical work, but the narrative guide explicitly says not to assume C and Saafiya are always the same playable character.
- **[L]** C's separate Sunday opening revolves around the **Chen family**, their **Home & More** store in **Pine Ridge Mall**, workers including Ismail, and a deteriorating civil-unrest situation. Mrs. Chen, Mr. Chen and son Wei have their own arcs. The opening store layout has approved artwork and should retain its existing gameplay reference independently.
- **[L]** Current native *Pineridge Crisis — Saafiya's Revenge* has playable **Upper Pineridge**, **House One / barricaded house**, and the feeder-road/main stage. Existing implemented chapter transitions and encounters are game facts, **not proof that every prose continuity branch is canon**.
- **[P/O]** Mall barricades, separation from a person Saafiya seeks, a taxi-related ambush, and future routes involving C, Russian, Domino, Justin, Fayeem and the Chen family were explored at different times. Their order, cast identification and causes remain open.
- **[L]** Existing sound/comic/game narrative direction includes motion-comic scenes, large expressive busts for dialogue, mood-sensitive gothic lettering, contemplative "pillow shots", a heartbeat metaphor, consequences beyond a simple morality meter, and industrial/darksynth plus restrained ambient/folk textures. Full archived narrative guide is linked below.

A draft relocation scene places Heather and Saafiya in an old grey **Renault 5**, carrying two suitcases and one additional bag, listening to Sandra's **"(I'll Never Be) Maria Magdalena"** on cassette; a **Princeton cigarette** and **glass-ball metaphor** enrich their complex conversation. ## Third-pass additions: visual scene variants and naming conflicts (28 November 2026)

- **[D/O]** An earlier mall approach names **Rivermere Mall** and **Mr Fahim**, whereas later sources use **Pine Ridge Mall**, **Fayeem**, the Chen store and staged Upper Pineridge roads. Neither pair of similar names is automatically identical; see [third-pass fragments](third-pass-fragments-2026-11-28.md).
- **[D/P]** An older **mid-2000s Cape Town poolside** six-panel evening illustration has Saafiya with a silver Motorola RAZR, a message **"BRB, got a visitor"** and a green emoticon; an ornate short-barrel revolver is another visual prop. This is a **visual scene option**, not automatic replacement of the inherited Beretta.
- **[A/P]** The **Gate 125** eight-panel dusk artwork shows a black Mustang approaching property 125, a light-blue Ford Escort and a suspicious man; varying images include a cautious drive-past, a cul-de-sac return and a dramatic engine-warning beat. Neither the geography nor a captioned travel-time is locked.
- **[A/O]** Different Fayeem-hunt comic drafts show **07:34** versus **10:24** on the phone and a coded-looking email sender versus a generic "burner" label. Preserve as alternate art text, not two established messages.
- **[D/A]** A separate sunrise balcony/yoga visual and an intimate, hopeful adult portrait-for-a-potential-lover concept exist, but have no confirmed recipient or chronology. The director favours sincerity and independent romantic initiative rather than status-driven courtship.
- **[O]** A 26 November question left the **final vehicle choice unsettled**, despite older literature repeatedly depicting a black Mustang (and one gameplay build showing a 1967 model). Maintain the design association without silently locking the year across all branches.
- **[D]** Heather's Renault scene includes advice to **"follow your heart"**, alongside the earlier glass-ball metaphor; the exact speech is not an approved final screenplay.

## Fourth-pass recovered family provenance and character-art decisions (28 Nov 2026)

- **[D/P]** Heather was additionally imagined as **adventurous, straight-haired, styled with 1960s/70s influences, possibly green- or steel-blue-eyed, and employed as a police officer**. Exact appearance, ancestry, duties and career chronology remain open. The director prefers Saafiya to remain **Faisal/Farrell's biological daughter**.
- **[D/P]** Saafiya's **maternal grandmother (Heather's mother)** may have owned an inexpensive **Rossi .38 Special**; this is a separate *family prop*, not a replacement for her **father's inherited engraved Beretta 84FS**, and not automatically the engraved revolver shown in an alternate poolside comic.
- **[D/O]** Saafiya's **left-handedness** was considered but **not finally fixed**. Left-biased pistol artwork is a visual design choice, not sufficient evidence of character handedness; the earlier poolside RAZR/revolver pose differs.
- **[D/V]** Earlier approved visual revisions involved correcting an overly mature-looking model, improving lip volume, eyes, hair and gait, trying a **painted anime/early-console face on 3D geometry**, and restoring a **low-cut sweetheart/V corset neckline**. V3.3's **1.72 m scale** and expression textures are technical details, not automatically a locked body height.
- **[V]** **C** has an independently approved (22 Nov 2026) Library art reference and should not inherit Saafiya's traits or vice versa merely because older code shares names and sprite systems.
- **[D/P]** Character-select concepts **Bruiser, Hitman, Gangster and Balanced**, traits with both upside/downside, persistent setup choices and individualized/possibly irreversible NPC story consequences are additional narrative design fragments.
