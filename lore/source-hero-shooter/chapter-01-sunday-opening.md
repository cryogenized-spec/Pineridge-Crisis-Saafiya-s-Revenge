# Hero Shooter — Chapter 1 opening continuity (working treatment)

**Status:** Story-design notes, not executable game logic. Keep implementation in public/play/, and do not copy this prose into game.js. This is a fictional scenario drawing on the director's recollections, not a factual history of real people or organisations.

## Core setting
- Sunday morning at a sizeable family-owned general-merchandise store in the Value Wing on L1 of Pine Ridge Mall.
- Mr. Chen's shop is **Home & More** (approved). The visual design for the L1 sales floor is confirmed in [the approved shop artwork brief](../art/home-and-more-shop-approved.md). "Golden Dragon" is a deprecated placeholder in legacy chapter material.
- Store contains shelves with varied household goods, electronics, appliances, tills, partial stock pallets, a water dispenser, a private administrative office for Mrs. Chen, a workers' canteen, a secure stockholding room and a rear service access.
- L1 service corridor and a freight lift connect to receiving/dispatch on G; staff move wrapped pallets with a pallet jack. The whole mall footprint is intentionally **not size-capped** during architectural planning.
- Partial LED/fluorescent lighting, as the store is closed to ordinary Sunday retail traffic. Working/receiving aisles are lit; other rows are dimmer.

## Family and associates
- C is a longstanding family friend with niche sourcing/import connections. She volunteers to help during the uncertain closure, not as a formally assigned guard.
- Mr. Chen follows social-media groups and calls other business owners, trying to judge whether to close. He is firm with his employees, not a caricature.
- Mr. Chen's son is fluent in English, business-oriented and competent with the premises. He uses the shop's keyed roller-shutter control when closure is ordered.
- Mrs. Chen handles business administration and payroll in a separate office. Her given name remains unsettled.
- Four volunteer staff present on Sunday (provisionally three men and one woman), accustomed to working in pairs or small groups. Each has their own concerns, family, vocabulary and tasks.

## Exploration and social interactions
- Allow genuine free exploration before crisis escalation. Employees work in short, varying loops: move a pallet, check an invoice, fetch cartons, talk, return to work.
- Workers who share isiZulu speak ordinary, informal isiZulu to one another. C doesn't understand it; literal dialogue bubbles can be shown without pretending she understands. Dialogue should be iteratively cross-checked with independent AI language reviews and kept grounded in each individual's voice. External human auditing is not a release gate.
- Optional: C offers to help Mrs. Chen distribute sealed payslip envelopes labelled by hand. Each employee confirms their name; player chooses the correct envelope from a compact inventory. If unfinished, the staff collect their own payslips at closure.
- Optional: C brings Mrs. Chen water from a dispenser. No experience points required; the action is remembered.
- Interactions focus one person at a time (direction + distance); nearby alternatives selectable on keyboard/mobile.

## Escalation beats (relative, not rigid quest timers)
1. Shop packing; C speaks to the son and Mrs. Chen; staff share ordinary news and increasingly worrying reports.
2. Exterior crowd noise grows gradually. Other retailers begin closing. Mr. Chen cross-checks increasingly troubling messages.
3. Mr. Chen orders closure. Son lowers the front roller shutter. Staff finish, collect payslips as needed and depart.
4. Transport crew in G receiving loses contact; a separate fictional betrayal/violent incident later accounts for the missing vehicle and goods. **Do not** disclose the incident immediately to C or the family.
5. Mr. Chen remains briefly in his office, anxious about the vehicle. His son asks C to fetch Mrs. Chen from administration and gather in the secure stockholding room.
6. C escorts Mrs. Chen through the familiar aisles to the son. Mr. Chen is still separated.
7. Mrs. Chen contacts her husband on speakerphone. The shutter/frontage sustains impacts, and the situation deteriorates. The store is not an invulnerable fortress.
8. Game returns control for a human-focused objective: account for the family and employees, determine a safe route out. Do not force unfinished payslip errands during the crisis.

## Presentation and systems
- Short letterbox cinematics using existing sprites, camera choreography and !/? emotes, plus optional illustrated WebP art cards for select emotional beats.
- C portrait bottom-left; conversation partner bottom-right; monologue C only.
- No magical healing. Field care stabilizes continued in-game health loss but does not restore lost health. Hydration/food assist recovery and stamina; partial rest only between genuinely linked chapters, with lasting condition preserved.
- Player notices environmental changes in areas they explored in the peaceful opening.
- Fictional political names under consideration: AAN (public movement) and ALF (radical splinter). Keep motives individual and distinguish civilian crowds from criminal actors.
