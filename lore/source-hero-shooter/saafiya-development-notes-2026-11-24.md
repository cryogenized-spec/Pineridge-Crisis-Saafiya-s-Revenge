# Saafiya / Sophia — expanded character and family development (24 November 2026)

> **Purpose:** Preserve the director's November 2026 brainstorming for *Hero Shooter* without prematurely making every possibility canon.
> **Status key:** **[D]** direct creative direction stated by the director; **[P]** proposed interpretation/scene awaiting approval; **[O]** open continuity decision. These notes supplement, rather than replace, [Saafiya's established biography](saafiya-history.md).
> **Spoilers:** Family tragedy, relationship deception, organised crime, possible character endings.
> **Setting:** Fictional story inspired in part by South African circumstances. Do not assert fictional allegations about real people or institutions.

## Canon safeguards and name reconciliation

- **[D]** The protagonist is referred to as **Saafiya**, **Sophia**, and **Sofia** in discussions. The latest idea links a birth name to the Greek-derived *Sophia* (“wisdom”), with an anglicised school spelling, but the director has **not** finally reconciled that proposal with existing canon, which currently specifies **Saafiya (“Fay”)**. Do **not** automatically replace the established story name or rename existing assets. *Sofia* and *Saafiya/Safiya* have different linguistic origins; a name change, adopted name, or correction must be decided explicitly.
- **[D]** Her father was born **Faisal Rasool** and later adopted **Farrell** as a chosen name. Whether Farrell became his legal first name and whether he retained *Rasool* as a surname remain **[O]**.
- **[D]** This family/backstory material belongs chiefly to the **late 2000s** during her Grades 10–12 years. Do not accidentally place her school years in the 1990s; the father's earlier professional/religious development may extend from the 1980s through the 1990s.
- **[D]** The story's **origin/basis is now intended to be KwaZulu-Natal**, with later expansion into Gauteng and other regions. This is a **setting-direction update**, not an automatic retcon of the already documented Johannesburg visit, 125 Copperlane lead, Pine Ridge Mall or Home & More chapter. Establish geographical transitions and dates before editing scenes.
- **[P]** Artistic resemblance between the darkly dressed, black-haired, powerful-vehicle-riding women of *Deep Steppes* and *Hero Shooter* is a recurring design motif; it is **not** proof of a connected fictional universe.

## Farrell / Faisal Rasool — father

### Appearance and manner
- **[D]** Large, broad and stocky, with short, slightly bristly or wiry black hair greying with age; his bearing recalls some older South African cricket personalities. Reserved, fairly formal, traditionally English-professional mannerisms, measured speech, discipline, practical humour. Not necessarily an English accent.
- **[D]** Born and raised Muslim. Over years he became more personally questioning, less observant, and less invested in institutional religion, while likely remaining a theist or uncertain universalist rather than declaring himself straightforwardly atheist.
- **[D]** Among his influences were work relationships with individual Jewish colleagues/mentors who valued argument, questioning and independent judgment; he noticed the practical worth of those habits and adopted aspects himself. Experiences or concerns around prejudice, Middle Eastern politics and the Muslim Brotherhood may have contributed to his dissatisfaction with organised religious-political identification.
- **Narrative guardrail:** His experiences belong to specific people and circumstances. Do not imply all Jewish people think alike, all Muslims forbid intellectual questions, or Muslim political organisations represent Islam as a whole. The traditions of inquiry in both religions are diverse. His decision to distance himself from mosque attendance and scheduled prayer is individual, not a general claim about religious practice.
- **[D]** His distancing is gradual: changing prayer/mosque habits, questioning ideas previously left unexamined in his upbringing, learning which discussions are best kept private, adopting *Farrell* as an expression of a different personal and professional identity.

### Professional arc
- **[D]** Commercial, logistics, procurement, warehousing, operations or supply-chain work suits his character; **exact employer and industry remain [O]**.
- **[D]** He advances not through implausible genius but accumulated, useful **B- and C-grade improvements**: consumables replenishment, fewer emergency requests, regular purchasing arrangements, workflow improvements, better SOPs, fewer cross-department interruptions and wasteful hand-offs.
- **[D]** A formative senior employer/mentor encourages initiative within one's legitimate remit: *Know what you have to do; do it. If you make a mistake, explain what you did and why, apologise, fix it, learn, and do not repeat it.* This is accountability, not limitless authority.
- **[D]** He receives growing responsibility because the small improvements show competence. His advancement should reflect the remaining social and professional barriers of South Africa's transition years without flattening his colleagues into caricatures.
- **[P]** He may ultimately spot and report procurement irregularities or corruption, leading to his murder. An investigation and possible suppression of evidence could become part of a larger conspiracy. The exact employer, scheme and persons responsible are **not established**.

### Family and loss
- **[D/P]** His wife, Saafiya's mother, has an Afrikaans-speaking Cape Flats background and police/K9-related experience. She could be physically formidable, short-tempered and historically prone to harsh physical discipline. She must remain an individual, not a stereotype of police officers or mothers.
- **[D]** As Farrell grows in outlook, he increasingly intervenes to restrain excessive discipline and give the child more scope to explain herself, make decisions and take responsibility. He remains exacting about manners, honesty and accountability.
- **[P]** One potent scenario: he is shot dead at close range at a traffic light (“robot”) after coming from work, perhaps because he had witnessed and reported wrongdoing. Whether Saafiya saw the murder, whether there was an official cover-up, and whether Russian connects to it are **[O]**.
- **[D]** Saafiya has seen dead bodies before; the father's death remains profoundly formative. Familiarity with mortality does not mean she is unaffected by killing.
- **[P]** His habits of inquiry survive in his daughter: her later investigations into art, meaning, paganism and spiritual questions follow a different path but spring from a similar refusal to accept simplistic answers.

## From Cape Flats to Cape Town — move after her father's death

### Reason and arrangement
- **[D]** After the death, Saafiya struggles emotionally and becomes more combative; school disciplinary trouble may approach expulsion. Her mother considers a different educational environment and, after condolence telephone calls with family and practical deliberation, arranges for her to stay with an aunt in Cape Town.
- **[D]** The decision takes **days**, including budgeting and logistics; it is not an impulsive overnight transfer. The aunt considers it on an **exam pad with pen/pencil**, doing figures, lists, drawings, layouts and sensible brainstorming.
- **[D]** Saafiya's mother funds her **school fees and boarding/accommodation costs** from available family resources; the aunt may offer a family-discount rate, not free indefinite board. The mother proposes paying roughly **two school terms (half a school year) in advance**, to show commitment without risking a full year's prepaid fees before the arrangement is tested. Four school terms is a more South African convention than calling these school “semesters.”
- **[P]** Farrell was careful with savings and might have left a will or testamentary educational provision; exact estate distribution, marriage regime and any discovery of hidden savings require legal and story decisions. Do not claim all his assets automatically pass to the widow.

### Aunt profile
- **[D]** An older, capable businesswoman, perhaps in her fifties, divorced several years earlier. She owns and manages her own accommodation/guest-lodging property and understands business, accounts and tenancy discipline.
- **[D]** Several adult children are at university or beyond school; one younger son (“late lamb”) is in Grade 11, perhaps boarding at another private school. His boarding circumstances and exact relationship with Saafiya are **[O]**.
- **[D]** Presentation: attractive, practical clothes such as a blouse with a cardigan or outer layer, costume-jewellery turquoise/black necklace, perhaps understated gold rings (not necessarily wedding rings), purposeful and composed. Affection is expressed through preparation and practical generosity, not grand declarations.
- **[P]** A marriage **out of community of property**, potentially with accrual excluded by antenuptial contract, could plausibly help her preserve premarital assets after divorce; this is a suggested legal backstory, **not** an established universal rule or absolute shield.

### Property and geography
- **[D]** A substantial Victorian/Edwardian-era home, painted or detailed in white, modernised and maintained, with separate cottages or guest units and a **communal pool**; the aunt's own house and rental accommodation share the grounds.
- **[P]** **Tamboerskloof** currently leads as a plausible Cape Town location; **Oranjezicht** is a serious alternative. A residential area outside the immediate CBD but with practical routes to school fits better than an isolated thatch-roof tourist lodge. Exact suburb, street and school commute remain **[O]**.
- **[D]** Tenants may use the pool without an onerous curfew, but noise and parties are regulated: quiet after approximately 22:00, no disruptive midnight/weekend parties, and consideration for other residents.
- **[D]** The aunt has a smaller **white-painted, carefully prepared cottage** ready for Saafiya: bed, clean bedding, study desk, cupboards and storage, functional lighting, curtains, perhaps some touches selected for a teenage girl. The welcome should be warm but restrained.
- **[P]** An arrival cutscene can dwell on keys passed to Saafiya, the fresh paint, a prepared desk, sunlight on bedding, the pool and ordinary sounds of the property. The preparation says “you are expected and welcome” without needing an exposition speech.

## School years (Grades 10–12, late 2000s)

- **[D]** A more modern, comparatively gentle, disciplined, academically oriented school culture. Earlier notes describe a mostly white/Jewish social milieu with fewer learners from other backgrounds; these are character recollections and **a fictional school's precise makeup remains open**, not a general claim about all Cape Town institutions.
- **[P]** A **fictional independent English-medium school** is preferable to attributing invented school incidents to a real institution. Afrikaans as a First Additional Language is plausible; she remains proficient in Afrikaans and may use it privately or when emotionally engaged.
- **[D]** She adapts surprisingly well, genuinely enjoys the more civil social environment, makes friends and appreciates that arguments often end quickly, are forgiven and are not endlessly escalated.
- **[D]** She remains unbeaten in occasional **fistfights**, yet the fights are shorter, controlled and not glorified by peers. Her schoolmates are generally unimpressed by boasting, swaggering, needless shouting or aggression. She learns that strength and dominance need not be social currency.
- **[D/P]** She initially walks to school; over time classmates or their families offer safe lifts or arrange pickups. Her daily routine becomes more comfortable.
- **[P]** The first year should progress through arrival, tentative friendships, school routines, cottage life, the aunt's practical household standards, computer use and quiet reflection before the more elaborate Ambrosia developments. Allow genuinely happy days and unremarkable domestic scenes.

### A personal computer
- **[D]** The aunt obtains a useful **second-hand desktop** for her niece, thinking computers are a modern necessity for a youngster and for education, even if she doesn't know the girl's recreational tastes.
- **[P]** Period-plausible circa 2007–09 machine: ex-office Pentium 4, CRT screen, Windows XP (Windows 2000 possible as legacy), CD-ROM/CD-RW, small hard drive. **There was no Windows 96.** Quake or Duke Nukem 3D may come via friends, old discs or CD-ROM; CD software was common by that period, with floppy disks more fitting for smaller/older files.
- **[P]** Homework, music exchange, early social messaging, internet research, diaries/poetry and art make the computer her first private intellectual space. Do not treat any exact installed game as settled.

## Ambrosia — the girls' informal boudoir circle

- **[D]** **Ambrosia** is the favoured simple name for the informal girls' club; “Victoria's Boudoir” was an alternative thought but has less appeal. Its classical association with the gods' nourishment/immortality can become meaningful without the founders planning a complicated symbolism.
- **[D]** A warm, private **reading, beauty and makeup circle**—not necessarily an official school organisation. Friends read books, experiment with makeup, exchange music, talk about friendships, literature, religion, philosophy and mythology. Sometimes a member's room or a pleasant private lounge serves as the meeting place.
- **[D]** Saafiya's gothic styling may begin through another goth girl who is somewhat marginal to the prevailing school fashions but is not automatically lonely or antisocial. Through friendship and makeup, Saafiya adopts the aesthetic naturally rather than being abruptly “converted.”
- **[D]** The music leads to folklore, then spiritual inquiry: gothic/dark music, Celtic folk, modern pagan folk and Norwegian sounds. Period-appropriate reference artists for research: **Faun** (Renaissance 2005, Totem 2007), **Omnia** (Pagan Folk 2006), **Gåte** (early 2000s); **Wardruna's debut arrives in 2009**, so should not feature as a widely known 2007 starting point.
- **[D]** Her eventual interest is in **paganism, nature spirituality, folklore, Wicca or related questioning**, **not an automatic Satanism storyline**. She may explore ideas while remaining sceptical, inward-looking and not committed to a single set of claims.
- **[D]** A defining private intuition: **“I might be part of something larger.”** This should arise quietly from grief, contemplation, reading, beauty and possible coincidence, not from an instant supernatural revelation.
- **[P]** Potential reflective flashback: a trivial debate about immortality or the meaning of “ambrosia” at the club, a joke, laughter, followed by Saafiya's lingering gaze and a quiet exterior *pillow shot* on her way home.
- **[P]** Astrological imagery such as **Pisces and its two fish** might suit her symbolic self-understanding, but the birth date and zodiac sign remain **[O]**; astrology is character symbolism, not a factual determinant of personality.

## An older acquaintance and the question of authority

- **[D]** At **18 or older**, Saafiya is introduced by **two young women friends** to a man around **23**, mature in demeanour and living a stable everyday life. He has **short, sharply styled dyed-black hair, green eyes, black eyeliner and paired snakebite lip piercings**.
- **[D/P]** First meeting at a public anime/cosplay expo, perhaps with Saafiya dressed as an anime character. This is **not especially his scene**; the two friends have another reason for the introduction, still **[O]**.
- **[D]** Only later, in private, does she discover that the two friends refer to him as their **master** and express deference by kneeling. They kept this aspect of their lives discreet.
- **[D]** Saafiya does **not** immediately join or accept the arrangement. The revelation strikes her forcefully and takes **days or weeks** to contemplate. She asks what it means and how their independence, trust and freely chosen submission coexist.
- **Writing safeguard:** If the plot explores adult dominance/submission, make participation genuinely voluntary, revocable and bound by respect; do not equate the relationship with paganism or depict uncertainty as consent. At present, his name, motives, influence, relation to any tattoo artist, and long-term role are **[O]**.
- **[O]** The **collar** worn by Saafiya is a longstanding gothic fashion motif, but whether it later acquires personal or relationship significance remains unresolved. Do not silently define it.

It occupies the **left upper back and shoulder blade**, made larger, taller, sweeping and more **elegant, elongated, feminine and sensual** than the source.
- **[D]** Ink: intricate black/grey blackwork, graceful wing membranes, refined hooked lines, stippled shading, dark negative space, taper toward mid/lower back; **not** a generic symmetrical angel-wing pair or heavy tribal graphic.

## Psychological arc and narrative potential

- **[D]** Saafiya's past includes exposure to death, occasional fistfights and a sense of physical confidence. Nevertheless, **her first lethal action can still be psychologically momentous**. Ability, willingness, self-justification and remorse are different matters.
- **[D]** She can be obsessive and restless when Fayeem disappears, unable to let go of a disturbing clue. Russian gives her consequential intelligence **in person**, implying that Fayeem may be connected to the mall or a deeper truth; she goes to investigate for herself.
- **[D]** At the mall she encounters **Ernest Meintjies**, street name **Domino**, a physically imposing man. His loyalties, moral character and precise function are **[O]**; avoid treating the imposing exterior as proof of stupidity.
- **[P]** An early confrontation with a hostile crowd could be a scripted/interactive cinematic of hesitation, disbelief, fear and escalating danger. How and when Saafiya crosses into lethal action matters. Distinguish an immediate threat from an unarmed or fleeing person; the consequences of harming a non-threatening person are substantially different. Do not force that into a supposedly free player choice.
- **[D]** A possible internal anchor upon player control: **“I need to proceed. Must find Fayeem.”** Her motives include love/fear, suspicion, anger, anxiety and a compulsion to discover what Russian is hiding.
- **[D]** Interacting with casualties and aftermath can prompt context-specific observations, regret, melancholy, revulsion or later emotional numbing, with variation reflecting actual choices rather than kill-count alone.
- **[D/P]** Her choices may increase affinity with Russian's methods, draw lasting attention from law enforcement, damage legitimacy and affect competing criminal or political factions. Corrupt actors might condone some violence while other authorities investigate; no universal blanket immunity.
- **[D]** A catastrophic end route titled or themed **“But None of Us Will Win”** sees her become Russian's successor, descend into brutal authoritarian/anarchic fragmentation and lose her own life. Divided communities, autonomous enclaves, predatory criminal/political brokers and exploitative foreign investment reveal the supposed victory to be ruin. This is a **tragic consequence path**, not a reward for harming as many people as possible.
- **[D]** Later chapters may move from 2021 KZN disorder into organised crime, local power struggles, corruption, Gauteng, and transnational interests. Treat people of every ethnic group as individuals; criminality cannot be assigned on demographic grounds.
