**Captured 28 November 2026.** This is the third sweep across the current conversation, retrieved earlier conversation context, the archived \`Hero-shooter\` story materials and available named visual-art descriptions. It is an **additive archival document**. It does **not** supersede the [unified character bible](saafiya-unified-notes.md), [Sarah/Ambrosia notes](sarah-ambrosia-dark-mountain.md), [Farsha notes](farsha-character-fragments.md), [first recovery](recovered-fragments-2026-11-28.md) or [continuity register](continuity-register.md).

**Provenance:** **[D]** user/director-specified story or character direction; **[P]** possible plot, visual or worldbuilding version; **[A]** source-art/prompt **depiction only** (not necessarily approved narrative); **[H]** recalled real-world social/community experiences used as inspiration; **[I]** assistant-inferred connective idea, **not** director canon; **[O]** open conflict, date or classification. When the same detail is both a user choice and a visual-only depiction, its *scope* matters: confirming a panel does not automatically confirm the event happened in the final story.

## 1. Sarah's additional private-life fragments

- **[D/P] Nocturnal habits.** An earlier conception portrayed Sarah as often awake late into the night or into the early morning, engaging with fantasy/online friends and creative interests. This helps explain her role in introducing Saafiya to the social hours and rhythms of Dark Mountain; **not** a fixed sleep schedule.
- **[D/P] Substance use.** Earlier ideation included secret **marijuana smoking** as well as smoking cigarettes. The more recent school drawing-spot story clearly retains discreet cigarette smoking; whether the marijuana detail remains part of the final story, its frequency or context, are **[O]**. Record as an imperfect/private behaviour without glamorising it, presuming addiction or treating it as a prerequisite for insight.
- **[D/P] Social networks.** Sarah has many male acquaintances as well as female friends and moves freely among different groups. Her wide acquaintance network doesn't prove a particular romantic history; social ease can coexist with selective emotional intimacy.
- **[D/P] A possible BB/Red Ryder-style air rifle** appears as an older possession/prop suggestion. Its owner, setting and relevance are **[O]**; do not import it into an action scene or turn it into an instructional detail.
- **[D/P] Folklore searches.** A richly illustrated childhood encyclopedia, received around age six, may have encouraged Sarah to explore wooded places looking for or imagining the fairies, elves and other beings in its pages. This childhood curiosity and her present-day intelligence can coexist without requiring she literally found supernatural beings.
- **[D/P] The ceremonial dagger** may have been a commissioned, unusual-looking black object with distinctive markings. An earlier scene explores a subjective tingling or energy sensation when Sarah or Saafiya concentrates on or holds it. **Whether this is a genuine supernatural response within the final fiction remains [O]**. No construction, testing or real-world practice instructions are intended.
- **[D] Her mother and grandmother.** Sarah's private domestic pressures (mother's past suicide attempt and alcohol misuse) coexist with a more expressive, permissive, 1980s-influenced outward household image. Sarah resists humiliating her mother or gossiping, while remaining entitled to seek appropriate help. Her father's location/status is not settled.

## 2. Farsha's route from school isolation to Dark Mountain — missing concrete steps

- **[D] Silence as avoidance, not mystic composure.** Farsha's school silence follows ridicule of her strong Transvaal Afrikaans accent in an English-medium environment. She withdraws to escape attention and to fade into the background; do **not** interpret this as cultivated mindfulness or Sarah-like inner calm.
- **[D/P] Library chess scene.** Boys at a library play using a **roll-up chess board** and improvised pieces kept in an **ice-cream tub**. Their talk, phones or informal use of GPRS/MXit creates an accessible introduction to the mobile-chat world.
- **[D/P] Phone progression.** Farsha may first use or borrow **her mother's phone**, eventually receiving a **Nokia from her father**, enabling more private and regular participation in Dark Mountain. The exact Nokia model and dates remain unspecified.
- **[D/P] Language and self-expression.** Written English and typing become easier or more fluent for her than speaking confidently in a school setting; the anonymity of the handle \`farsha\` reduces immediate exposure of her accent.
- **[D/P] Midnight roleplay.** Earlier discussions pictured her remaining awake at night for Dark Mountain battles, occult-flavoured text roleplay and in-character 'incantations'. This is **in-character performance unless another fictional scene deliberately establishes otherwise**; the phrase should not be treated as proof of real-world magical efficacy.
- **[O]** Farsha's online initiation, clan membership, and confrontation with Saafiya are not yet integrated into a single chronology. Her group originated **online**, contrasting with Ambrosia's **offline-first** founding.

## 3. Dark Mountain's lived cultural texture — supplemental history, not automatic fictional canon

- **[H]** Clan tags were meaningful identifiers. Participants might present as vampires, lycans/werewolves, mages, or followers of other esoteric/'left-hand-path' personas. Different participants treated those identities with different degrees of literalism, irony or symbolic commitment.
- **[H]** The roleplay world included competitive fights, long-term rivalries, clan hierarchy, alliances, manipulation or sowing distrust, unspoken etiquette, respect, and taboo. **Reputation was partly a social product of writing style, chosen emoticons, consistent participation and contribution**, not simply contest wins.
- **[H]** Offline friendships and meetings could make online bonds unusually meaningful. Established MSN/Yahoo/forum users brought old contacts, typing conventions and chat habits to newer mobile spaces; some were in their forties or fifties while others were teenagers or young adults.
- **[H]** The director's personal vampire-lord roleplay included recruiting participants drawn to unusual typing style, conversational presence and 'energy', with roleplay and real romantic/social attachment sometimes overlapping. He reports further personal experiences interpreted as remote perception. Preserve as firsthand recollection and influence on the fiction, not independent external verification of those powers.
- **[H]** The old community's apparent openness or relative innocence should not become an unsupported blanket claim that earlier internet spaces were uniformly civil or safe. The director also recalls aggression, betrayal and rivalry.
- **[D]** The fictional Ambrosia coven may contain practitioners whose unusual gifts are genuinely real **within the setting**. Its identity is not exclusively a supernatural school, and Saafiya's primary abilities remain practical rather than magical.

- **[D/O]** A 20 November storyline discussion names **Rivermere Mall** as a destination where obstructing looters interfere with Saafiya's attempt to get to **Mr Fahim**; other early drafts retain a street-side barricade and nearby parked vehicle.
- **[L/O]** Later work instead refers to **Pine Ridge Mall**, Saafiya's search for **Fayeem**, the Chen family's Home & More store, and newer **Upper Pineridge / House One / feeder road** levels in the Godot project.
- **[O]** It has **not** been decided whether Rivermere and Pine Ridge are alternate names for the same fictional site, distinct malls, a renamed draft, or unrelated scenes. Likewise, **Mr Fahim and Fayeem** might or might not represent the same intended person. **Do not merge based on proximity or phonetics alone.**
- **[P]** The 'Saafiya street-side, blocked off, searching for someone in the mall' opening can coexist in the fragment archive with the later **Johannesburg/Copperlane** email opening and **KZN-first** narrative direction. Their sequence and geography remain **[O]**.
